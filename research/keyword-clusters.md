# Keyword research: clusters, volumes and page mapping

Source: DataForSEO via OpenSEO, United States, English, pulled September 15, 2026. Volumes are monthly averages and estimates. KD is keyword difficulty 0 to 100. CPC is the Google Ads cost per click, which is the best proxy for how much a click is worth to a merchant services company. Raw export: `keywords-raw.csv` (823 rows).

Read this as demand evidence, not a promise. A page that ranks for the fan-out set around a keyword tends to earn more than the headline volume suggests, because AI answers pull from pages that cover the whole cluster.

## How to read the CPC column

Merchant services keywords have some of the highest CPCs on Google. "Credit card processor for small business" is $94 a click. "Merchant payment processing" is $112. "Quickbooks desktop credit card processing" is $135. When a click is worth that much to advertisers, an organic page that converts even 2% of visitors to calls is worth building even at 50 searches a month.

## What not to chase

Navigational volume for other brands: clover login (110,000), square login (368,000), authorize.net login (12,100), stripe payment processing (9,900). The intent is to log in to an account you do not control. Ranking for them is unlikely and would not convert.
