# Payment gateway cluster: keywords and page map

Second research pass, September 15, 2026. Seeds: payment gateway, nmi gateway, virtual terminal, level 3 processing, payment gateway for woocommerce, plus a 150-term metrics batch. Raw export: `keywords-gateways-raw.csv` (362 rows). DataForSEO via OpenSEO, US, English.

The cluster exists for two reasons. First, topical authority: a site that covers gateways completely (basics, pricing, features, every major gateway, every platform, every migration) gets cited by AI answers for the whole topic, not just the pages that rank. Second, outbound: every gateway on your call lists now has a page you can text during the call that answers what the prospect will search afterward.
