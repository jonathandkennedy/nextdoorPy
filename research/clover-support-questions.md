These are existing Clover merchants, many of them frustrated, many of them on someone else's pricing. A support page that gives them the number and the answer, then explains that Clover cannot change their rate, is a direct path to statement analyses.

## The ten questions merchants call about

Compiled from Clover help center topic frequency, dealer troubleshooting guides, and the long-tail queries above. Each is answered on /clover/support with a link to the full page.

1. How do I refund or void a transaction? (void before batch, refund after; /clover/refunds-and-voids)
2. The printer is not printing / kitchen printer offline. (paper, Diagnostics queue, Devices refresh; /clover/troubleshooting)
3. The device says offline / will not connect. (router, LTE, offline mode; /clover/offline-mode)
4. When does the batch close and when do I get paid? (nightly auto-close; deposits next business day; short deposit is a processor question)
5. How do I add or change a menu item or modifier? (Inventory app; /clover/how-to-use-clover)
6. How do I add an employee and set permissions? (Employees app, roles, PINs)
7. How do I adjust a tip after the sale? (Transactions, Add Tip, before batch)
8. A card declined / tap is not working. (bank decline vs reader; hold flat two seconds; restart)
9. The screen is frozen / device will not turn on. (power cycle; Flex hold power; dock a dead Flex)
10. How do I get daily sales, tips and tax reports? (Reporting app, dashboard export, QuickBooks connector)

Plus the question Clover cannot answer: why did my rate go up. Routed to the statement analysis.

## Who-to-call split (used on the support page)
