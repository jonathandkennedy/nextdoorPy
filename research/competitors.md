2. /store ranks #5 for "clover system pos".
3. /store/p/clover-station-solo ranks #8 for "clover pos".
4. /blog/clover-pos-cost-pricing-2025-hardware-clover-fees-proc ranks #7 for "clover monthly fees".
5. Vertical pages: /clover-for-restaurants, /growthzilla-uzeli-salon-pos, /greta-grocery-store-pos.

Blog titles worth noting because they map to fan-out queries: "Clover vs. Toast for Small Restaurants in 2026", "Clover Station Duo vs. Clover Mini for Small Business", "Dual Pricing Compliance for Clover POS in 2026", "How to Choose a Clover POS Reseller in 2026", "How to Take Clover POS Payments During Internet Outages", "Clover Flex vs. Clover Mini for Dental Offices".

Lesson: a small dealer with one product focus (Clover), a page per device, a page per vertical, and a comparison blog beats national brands on the commercial Clover queries. That is the model. Nextdoor Payments' advantage over Limelight is dual pricing as the headline economic argument and a stronger local angle.

## AI Overview presence

Every one of the eight SERPs we pulled live (authorize.net alternatives, clover pos pricing, dual pricing credit card processing, authorize.net vs nmi, clover pos reseller, switch from authorize.net, best payment gateway for woocommerce, quickbooks credit card processing fees) had an AI Overview at position 1 and a People Also Ask block. The pages that get cited in those overviews are the ones with dated facts, tables and a direct answer near the top. That is the format every article on this site uses.
