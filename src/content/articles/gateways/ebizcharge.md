EBizCharge sells one thing well: payments that live inside the accounting system so that when a customer pays, the invoice closes itself. For finance teams that is the whole value. This page describes the product, how it is priced, and how to decide between it and lighter options.

## What EBizCharge is

Century Business Solutions' platform, branded as an embedded payment platform, operating as an ISO with sponsor banks including Wells Fargo, PNC Bank, U.S. Bank, Synovus Bank and Pathward. From the site as of September 2026:

- Native integrations with 100+ business systems: QuickBooks Online and Desktop, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Sage 100, Microsoft Dynamics GP, Business Central and Finance, Acumatica, Epicor, Oracle Fusion, SAP and others.
- Credit and debit card, ACH and eCheck processing; EMV terminal support.
- Email Pay (payment links), a customer payment portal, recurring billing and auto-pay.
- Virtual terminal and mobile pay.
- Tokenization for stored payment methods, cash application automation, fraud prevention, chargeback management.
- Sold direct, with demos, and through reseller, referral and ISV partner channels.

## Pricing

Not published. The site advertises flat-rate options ("pay the same percentage each month") and no hidden fees. What to get in writing:

| Line | Ask for |
|---|---|
| Transaction rate | Flat rate or interchange-plus, and the number |
| Per-item fee | Cards and ACH separately |
| Monthly platform or gateway fee | Including any per-integration fee |
| ACH pricing | Percentage and cap, or flat |
| Contract term | Initial term, auto-renewal, early termination fee |
| Setup and integration | One-time fees |

Flat-rate pricing is simple and, for a B2B merchant with a large commercial card share, usually more expensive than interchange-plus with Level 3. Ask whether interchange-plus is available.

## Who it fits

- Mid-size businesses running NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Dynamics, Acumatica or Epicor where the finance team wants payment posting, cash application and a customer portal inside the ERP.
- Businesses with a high invoice count where manual posting is a real cost.
- Companies that need one platform across several accounting systems.

## Who does not need it

A small business on QuickBooks that wants a pay link on invoices, ACH, and automatic sync. That job is done by lighter tools on interchange-plus, such as Hyfin, at lower all-in cost. The [QuickBooks fees page](/quickbooks/credit-card-processing-fees) covers that setup and the numbers.

## How to decide

Count invoices per month and estimate the hours spent posting payments. If it is dozens of hours, integration depth is worth a platform fee and EBizCharge belongs on the shortlist. If it is a few hours, choose on effective rate, because the integration gap between products narrows for simple QuickBooks workflows. Either way, put the rate, fees and term on one page and compute the all-in cost on last month's volume before signing anything.
