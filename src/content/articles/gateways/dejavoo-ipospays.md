Dejavoo is best known for its terminals: the countertop and wireless units that a large share of independent processors place. Less known is that Dejavoo also runs a gateway, iPOSpays, so a merchant with a Dejavoo terminal can take online, keyed, recurring and link payments on the same account with one report. We are a Dejavoo dealer. This page describes the gateway, how it fits with the hardware, and when we use it versus an independent gateway.

## What iPOSpays does

From Dejavoo's site as of September 2026:

- **Hosted payment page (checkout).** A payment page for websites that keeps card entry off your server.
- **Cloud POS virtual terminal.** Browser-based keyed and stored-card transactions.
- **Recurring payments.** Scheduled billing from tokenized cards.
- **Payment links.** Send a request by email or text; the customer pays on a hosted page.
- **Tokenization.** Stored cards for repeat customers.
- **Level 2 and 3 data enrichment.** Auto-fill so commercial card transactions qualify for lower interchange.
- **S.A.F.E. fraud protection.** Proactive transaction monitoring.
- **SPIn.** Dejavoo's turnkey API for POS software integration, described as a one-and-done integration.

Sold to ISOs, ISVs and resellers as part of Dejavoo's partner ecosystem. Merchants get it through a dealer.

## How it fits with the terminals

A Dejavoo terminal (the Z-series countertop units, the wireless and mobile units) is boarded to a merchant account and processor. iPOSpays sits on the same account for everything that is not a tap or dip at the counter. The benefit is one login and one reconciliation for in-store, online, keyed and recurring. The limitation is that iPOSpays is Dejavoo's gateway; if your website, practice management or invoicing software has a native integration to accept.blue, NMI or Authorize.net and not to iPOSpays, those are the better fit for the online side while Dejavoo stays on the counter.

## Dual pricing on Dejavoo

Dejavoo terminals support dual pricing configured at the processor level: the cash price and card price display on the terminal before the customer chooses, the receipt shows the price paid and tender type, and the merchant's processing cost on in-store sales is zero. It is the default configuration on terminals we place. For online and link payments, transactions are card-only and run at the card price; the disclosure rules are the same. Details and the comparison to surcharging are on the [dual pricing vs surcharging vs cash discount page](/dual-pricing/dual-pricing-vs-surcharging-vs-cash-discount).

## Pricing

Dealer-set, as with every partner-sold gateway. Through us the quote shows: terminal placement (free-equipment program where the merchant qualifies, or purchase), the dual pricing program, the gateway monthly and per-item fee if iPOSpays or an independent gateway is added, and interchange-plus processing for any card-not-present channel. Month to month.

## When we use iPOSpays vs an independent gateway

| Situation | Gateway |
|---|---|
| Dejavoo at the counter, occasional keyed or link payments, no cart | iPOSpays: one account, one report |
| Dejavoo at the counter plus a WooCommerce, Magento or BigCommerce store | accept.blue or NMI for the store, on the same merchant account |
| Invoicing synced to QuickBooks | Hyfin or accept.blue invoicing; Dejavoo for walk-ins |
| B2B with commercial cards | Either; both do Level 2 and 3 |
| Software with a SPIn integration | Dejavoo terminals driven by the software; gateway per the software's online integration |
