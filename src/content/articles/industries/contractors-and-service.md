Contractors and service businesses have a different payment problem from restaurants and stores. The ticket is large, the customer is not standing at a counter, and the money often shows up as a check three weeks later, or as a card payment on an emailed invoice at 2.99% or more. Here is the setup that fixes both.

## The two costs

**Slow payment.** A $6,000 HVAC install paid by check averages two to four weeks from invoice to deposit. Text-to-pay with a bank-transfer option gets most of those paid in days, because the customer pays from the text on their phone the evening they get it.

**Card fees on invoices.** Invoiced card payments are card-not-present and carry the highest rates. QuickBooks Payments charges 2.99% (April 2026), 3.5% if keyed. On $40,000 a month that is $1,000 to $1,400 a month. Interchange-plus pricing on the same volume typically lands at $700 to $850. A dual-priced invoice, where the customer chooses between a bank-transfer price and a card price, takes the business's cost to $0. The full QuickBooks fee breakdown is in [QuickBooks credit card processing fees](/quickbooks/credit-card-processing-fees).

## What we set up

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hyfin invoicing | Send invoices by text and email with a pay link. Card, ACH and eCheck. Recurring billing for maintenance plans. Deposits and progress payments. |
| QuickBooks sync | Two-way with QuickBooks Online and Desktop. Invoice goes out from your QuickBooks data; payment posts back to the invoice; deposit matches for reconciliation. |
| Dual-priced invoices | Bank-transfer amount and card amount shown side by side. Customer picks. Business nets the bank-transfer amount either way. |
| Field terminal | Clover Flex or Dejavoo wireless for on-site tap, dip and swipe with dual pricing. Optional. |
| Virtual terminal | Take a card over the phone from the office when needed. |
| Customer vault | Stored payment methods for repeat customers and recurring plans, tokenized. |

Nothing changes in QuickBooks. Same file, same customers, same invoice template and numbering.
