Station Duo is the Clover most restaurants and busy retail counters end up with, and the one most often photographed in Clover's marketing. This page covers what is in the box, what it costs direct versus through a dealer, and the one feature that makes it the right pick over the cheaper Solo for most counters.

## What the customer screen does

The second screen is not a luxury. It moves four things off your staff and onto the customer:

1. **Tap to pay.** The customer taps their own card or phone. Staff never handle it.
2. **Tips.** Preset percentages or amounts, chosen by the customer. Tip rates go up when the customer picks on their own screen.
3. **Signature and receipt choice.** Print, email, text, or none.
4. **Dual pricing display.** Both the cash total and the card total appear before the customer chooses how to pay. That is the disclosure that keeps a dual pricing program compliant, and it is why we do not recommend Solo for merchants running dual pricing at the counter. The mechanics are on the [dual pricing on Clover](/clover/dual-pricing) page.

## Who should buy it

- Full-service restaurants: Duo at the server station or host stand, Flex handhelds for the floor.
- Bars: tabs, split checks and tips on the customer screen.
- Quick service with lines: Duo at the counter, a Kiosk beside it once the line is a problem.
- Retail with barcodes: the built-in scanner and the customer display for two prices.

## Who should not

A coffee bar or small counter with one register is well served by a Mini at less than half the price. A staff-only station behind a bar where customers never face the screen can use a Solo plus a Flex. A business that needs to keep taking cards during internet outages should add a Flex on LTE, because the Station line is built for a continuous connection.

## Accessories worth having

A second cash drawer for a two-lane counter, a kitchen printer or Clover Kitchen Display for restaurants, a handheld barcode scanner for high-volume retail, and a Flex for pay at table or line busting. All placed or purchased with the Duo on one quote.
