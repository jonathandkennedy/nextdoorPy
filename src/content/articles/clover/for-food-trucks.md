A food truck's POS has to survive a parking lot with no Wi-Fi, a generator that browns out, a twelve-hour festival day, and a line that does not stop. Then it has to fit in a drawer. The Clover Flex does all of that, and it is the device we send out with most trucks. This page is the setup and the settings that keep it running.

## The setup

| Item | Choice | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| POS | Clover Flex Gen 4 | Battery, LTE, printer, camera; runs the whole menu |
| Second window | Second Flex | Same account and menu |
| Receipts | Printed from the Flex, or text and email on a Flex Pocket | Pocket is lighter, no printer |
| Cook's view | Kitchen printer or Kitchen Display inside the truck | For trucks where the cook cannot see the window Flex |
| Charging | Dock in the truck on inverter power; a second battery-charged Flex for long days | 8-hour rating; a 14-hour festival needs a swap or a dock break |
| Pre-orders | Clover Online Ordering page | Post the link before events |
| Cash | Cash drawer optional; many trucks use a lockbox and log cash sales on the Flex | Cash sales recorded for reporting |

The Flex's full specs are on the [Clover Flex page](/clover/flex).

## Connectivity and offline mode

The Flex connects over LTE by default. Enable offline payments in the Clover dashboard before you need them, set a per-transaction limit, and the Flex keeps taking cards when the signal drops, submitting them when it returns. Two rules: offline transactions are not authorized until they sync, so the limit should be an amount you can afford to lose on a declined card; and refunds need a live connection. Full detail on [Clover offline mode](/clover/offline-mode).

## Menu for a window

Few items, big buttons, modifiers for the choices that matter (protein, spice, sides, drinks), combos as one tap. Sold-out toggles for the item that runs out at 1pm. Daily specials as a category you edit from the dashboard on your phone.

## Tips

Tip prompt on the Flex screen at tap; presets by amount for small tickets rather than percentages. Tips report per shift for payout to the crew.

## Dual pricing at the window

The Flex shows the cash total and the card total; the customer picks. A sign at the window and a line on the menu board disclose it. On a $12 order the difference at 3.99% is 48 cents. A truck doing $25,000 a month in cards keeps about $700 a month that a processor would take. The rules and the comparison with a surcharge are on [dual pricing on Clover](/clover/dual-pricing).

## Cost

Flex placed at no charge for qualifying trucks, or purchased; never leased. Software: Counter Service plan at $59.95 a month, or Essentials at $14.95 for a simple menu with no online ordering. Processing $0 under dual pricing or interchange-plus. Month to month. The Flex ships pre-programmed with the menu loaded; a 15-minute call gets the crew running.
