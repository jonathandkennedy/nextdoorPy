The Mini is the Clover we place most often for businesses with one register and a small counter. It runs the same software as the big Station, takes every card type, prints receipts, and costs $849 list instead of $1,899. This page covers what it does, where it falls short of a Station, and the three ways it gets deployed.

## Specs, September 2026

| Item | Clover Mini Gen 3 |
|---|---|
| Screen | 8-inch high-resolution touchscreen |
| Printer | Built-in thermal receipt printer |
| Payments | EMV chip, swipe, NFC contactless (Apple Pay, Google Pay), PIN debit |

## Three ways to deploy it

**1. The only register.** Coffee shops, juice bars, bakeries, salons, barbers, dry cleaners, small boutiques, food counters, service front desks. Add a cash drawer through the hub if you take cash. The customer taps on the Mini's screen, which faces them when you turn it or sits on a swivel stand.

**2. Customer-facing device for a Station Solo.** The Solo has no customer tap. A Mini beside it becomes the customer's screen for tap, tip, signature, receipt and the dual pricing display. This is how a bar or service station gets Duo-like function from a Solo.

**3. Second lane.** A Duo at the main counter and a Mini at a second register, both on the same account, same menu, same reporting.

A common mistake is buying a Duo for a coffee shop. The Mini does the job, and the money saved covers a year of software.

## Offline mode

The Mini supports Clover's offline payment queue. Enable it in the dashboard under payments settings, set a per-transaction limit, and during an outage the Mini stores transactions encrypted and submits them when the connection returns. Two cautions: enable it before you need it, and remember that offline transactions are not authorized until they sync, so a declined card is your loss. The LTE radio is the better first line of defense. Details on the [Clover offline mode page](/clover/offline-mode).

## Cost through a dealer

Placed at no charge for qualifying merchants, or purchased outright; never leased. Software at Clover's published plan prices. Processing at $0 under dual pricing or on interchange-plus for merchants who want one posted price.
