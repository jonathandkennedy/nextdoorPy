## The Solo trap

The Solo is $100 cheaper than the Duo and lacks the customer tap. Almost every Solo installation then adds a Flex or Mini so customers can tap, which costs more than the $100 saved and puts two devices on the counter. The Solo is right only where the counter is genuinely staff-only. If you are not sure, it is the Duo.

## The Duo overbuy

A single-register cafe on a Duo is paying $1,050 more than the Mini for a second screen it uses and a scanner it does not. Under a placement program the hardware cost is the dealer's, not yours, but placement terms scale with equipment value. The Mini does the job.

## Dual pricing on each

Duo shows both totals on the customer screen. Mini shows them on its screen turned toward the customer. Solo shows them on the paired Flex or Mini. In every case the customer sees both prices before choosing tender, the receipt shows the price paid, and the merchant's processing cost is zero.
