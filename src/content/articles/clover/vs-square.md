## Where Square wins

- **Under about $5,000 a month.** The free reader and free software make it nearly costless to start, and the processing difference at that volume is small.
- **Speed.** An account in ten minutes with no salesperson.
- **One vendor for everything.** Square Appointments, Invoices, Online, Payroll, Loans, all in one login. Businesses that want an all-in-one and are fine paying Square's rate for it get real value.
- **Very small or very new businesses** that do not yet know their volume.

## Where Clover wins

- **Dual pricing.** The whole argument. A cafe doing $22,000 a month pays Square about $600 a month in processing. On Clover through us with dual pricing it pays $0. Over a year that is more than the price of every piece of Clover hardware on the counter. Square does not offer dual pricing or cash discount programs; it offers surcharging, which does not apply to debit and is restricted in California.
- **Hardware for real counters.** Station Duo's two screens, built-in printer, cash drawer and barcode scanner; Flex for pay at table; Kiosk for self-order. Square's Register and Terminal are capable but the lineup is thinner for full-service restaurants and bigger retail.
- **An underwritten merchant account.** Square holds funds and closes accounts after the fact when its models flag a business. A merchant account underwritten to you has that conversation up front.
- **Interchange-plus** for merchants who do not want two prices. Square is flat-rate only.
- **A person.** Through a dealer, someone who knows your setup answers the phone.

## The risks, both sides

**Clover through a dealer.** The dealer contract. Leases, three-year auto-renewing agreements, teaser rates, junk fees. Clover's hardware is excellent and Clover's own terms are fair; a bad dealer is what gives Clover a bad name. The eight questions to ask are on [Clover reseller vs direct](/clover/reseller-vs-direct).

**Square.** The aggregator model. Fast growth, a spike in refunds, a category Square dislikes, and funds are held for 90 days or the account is closed, with no underwriter to call. Businesses that have been through it do not go back.

## The decision

Under $5,000 a month, or you want to be live today with no conversation: Square. Above that, with a counter, a restaurant or a real retail floor: Clover through a dealer with month-to-month terms and dual pricing, and read the contract.
