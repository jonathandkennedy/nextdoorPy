Clover reviews online are a mess, and not because Clover is a mess. The same product is sold by Fiserv direct, by banks, and by thousands of dealers with their own pricing and contracts. A review of "Clover" is often a review of whoever sold it. We sell it, so read this knowing that, and use it to sort the reviews you find elsewhere.

## What merchants consistently praise

- **Hardware.** Station Duo, Mini and Flex are solid, fast and good-looking on a counter. Units run for years. The Flex's battery and printer make pay at table work.
- **Restaurant software.** Clover Dining for tables, modifiers and combos, tips, kitchen display, online ordering included on restaurant plans. Enough for a single location.
- **Ease of use.** Staff learn it in an hour. The interface is the reason many operators choose it over more capable but harder systems.
- **App Market.** Loyalty, gift cards, scheduling, accounting sync, delivery integrations, payroll partners. Broad.
- **Reporting.** Sales, items, employees, tips, by period and location, from the dashboard or phone.

## What merchants consistently complain about

- **App fees.** Each App Market addition carries a monthly fee, often $10 to $60. A restaurant that adds five apps has doubled its software bill. Buy the ones you will use.
- **Locked devices.** A Clover cannot be reprogrammed by another processor, and used units are usually unusable. This surprises people and it should be disclosed before purchase. Details on [used Clover and reprogramming](/clover/used-clover-and-reprogramming).
- **Inventory depth.** Purchase orders, vendors, case breaks and age verification are thin. High-SKU stores outgrow it.
- **Multi-location.** Workable for a few locations, weak for chains.
- **Support.** Clover's own support is adequate; the experience depends heavily on whether a dealer stands in front of it.
- **Outages.** Cloud POS outages happen. Offline mode and LTE backup limit the damage, but a Station without either is down when the internet is.

## What is not Clover's fault

Read a page of one-star reviews and sort them. A large share say some version of: the rate went up, there is a lease I did not understand, I was charged an early termination fee, nobody answers the phone. Those are the seller. Clover.com's own terms are month to month on processing with published rates; a dealer's terms are whatever the dealer wrote.

| Complaint | Whose fault |
|---|---|
