Toast and Clover are the two systems most independent restaurants end up choosing between, and they are built on opposite bets. Toast bets that a restaurant wants one vendor for everything and will accept Toast's processing to get it. Clover bets that a restaurant wants good hardware and software and the freedom to choose who processes its cards. We sell Clover. Here is the comparison with the places Toast wins left in.

## Worked example: 60-seat full-service restaurant

$60,000 a month in cards, 1,600 transactions, Station-plus-two-handhelds equivalent on each.

| | Toast Point of Sale plan | Toast Starter Kit, $0 hardware | Clover direct | Clover through us, dual pricing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Software | $69 | $0 | $89.95 | $89.95 |
| Hardware | About $1,000 to $2,000 | $0 upfront | About $3,400 | $0 placement |
| Processing | $1,494 + $240 = $1,734 | About $2,000 to $2,200 | $1,380 + $160 = $1,540 | $0 |
| Annual processing | About $20,800 | About $25,000 | About $18,500 | $0 |

Toast's $0 hardware offer is paid for in the processing rate, several times over, across a multi-year term. Nothing at Toast is free; it is moved into the rate.

## Where Toast wins

- **Multi-location groups.** Menu management, reporting and permissions across locations are native and excellent.
- **One vendor.** Payroll, scheduling, tip management, inventory and POS in one system with one support line. Restaurants that value that integration over cost should take Toast.
- **Complex kitchens.** Toast's KDS and routing are mature.
- **Restaurants that want Toast Capital or Toast's financing** and understand the cost.

## Where Clover wins

- **Processing choice, and dual pricing.** A single-location restaurant doing $60,000 a month keeps roughly $18,000 to $25,000 a year that would go to Toast processing. That number is larger than every other difference on this page. How it works at the table, with tips, is on [dual pricing on Clover](/clover/dual-pricing).
- **No lock-in.** Month-to-month terms through us; no long-term processing contract direct.
- **Hardware you can move between dealers** if the relationship goes bad, since Clover runs on many processors' programs.
- **Cost of software.** Counter Service at $59.95 covers a cafe or QSR fully.
- **A local person** for installs and support.

## What Clover lacks against Toast

Native payroll and scheduling (Clover uses App Market partners such as Homebase and ADP integrations). Multi-location depth. Toast's restaurant-specific hardware ecosystem. If your operation is three locations and growing, weigh those honestly.

## The decision

Under about 100 seats, single location, and you care about the processing line: Clover through a dealer with dual pricing. Multi-location, or you want payroll and scheduling in the POS and are willing to pay for it in processing: Toast. Either way, get the contract term and the effective processing rate in writing before the hardware shows up.
