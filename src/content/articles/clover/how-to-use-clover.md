Clover is easy to learn and full of small things nobody shows you. This is the working manual: the tasks that come up every day, each in a few steps. Device buttons vary slightly by generation; the app names and flow are the same on Station, Mini and Flex.

## Where things are

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| Register, or Dining on the Table Service plan | Ring sales, tabs, tables, split checks, discounts |
| Transactions | Find any payment: void, refund, reprint, add tip |
| Orders | Open, saved and completed orders; online orders |
| Inventory | Items, categories, modifiers, prices, tax, availability |
| Employees | Staff, roles, PINs, permissions, time clock |
| Reporting | Sales, items, tips, taxes, payments by tender, by period |
| Setup | Business info, receipts, tips, taxes, payments, printers, offline mode |
| Diagnostics | Printer status, connectivity, queue |
| Dashboard (web and phone app) | All of the above, plus exports and app management |

## Sales

**Ring a sale.** Register, tap items (modifiers prompt as you add), tap Pay, choose tender. Card: the customer taps, dips or swipes on the customer screen or Flex and picks a tip. Cash: enter the amount tendered; the drawer opens.

**Discount.** In the order, tap the item or the order total, Discount, choose or enter one. Permissions can restrict who can discount.

**Split a check.** In Dining, or in Register on the order screen: Split, by item, by seat, by amount or evenly. Each split pays separately.

**Start a tab.** Register or Dining: start the order, tap the tab option, name it or swipe the card to pre-authorize (if enabled on your account), save. Reopen from open orders to add rounds. Close with payment and tip.

**Hold and recall.** Save an order to the open orders list; recall it from Orders.

**Reprint a receipt.** Transactions, select the payment, Reprint or Send (email or text).

**Add a tip after the sale.** Transactions, select the payment, Add Tip, enter, confirm. Only before the batch closes.

**Void or refund.** Transactions, select the payment, Void (before batch) or Refund (after). Full steps on [Clover refunds and voids](/clover/refunds-and-voids).

## Menu and inventory

**Add an item.** Inventory, Items, Add: name, price, category, tax rate, attach modifier groups. Saves to every device within a minute or two.

**Add a modifier group.** Inventory, Modifiers, Add group (size, milk, temperature), add modifiers with prices, set minimum and maximum selections, then attach the group to items.

**Change a price.** Inventory, find the item, edit the price. For a whole category, use the dashboard's bulk edit.

**86 an item.** Inventory, item, toggle availability off. Comes off register, kiosk and online ordering. Turn it back on the same way, from the phone app if you are off site.

**Categories and printer routing.** Categories organize the register; printer routing (Setup, Printers, or in the category settings) sends each category to the right kitchen printer or display.

## Employees

**Add an employee.** Employees, Add: name, role, PIN. Roles: Employee, Manager, Admin, or custom roles with specific permissions.

**Permissions.** Edit the role: voids, refunds, discounts, opening the drawer, viewing reports, editing inventory. Cashiers should not have void and refund without a manager override.

**Time clock.** Setup, or the Time Clock app: staff clock in and out with their PIN; reports show hours.

**Tip reports.** Reporting, Tips, by employee and period, for payout.

## Closing the day

**Tips.** Adjust any printed-slip tips in Transactions before the batch.

**Batch.** Closes automatically at the set time. To close manually: Transactions or Closeout, Close Batch, confirm.

**Drawer.** Cash app or Closeout: count the drawer, record cash in and out, note the variance.

**Closeout report.** Reporting, Sales Overview for the day: sales by tender, tips, tax, discounts, refunds. Email it to yourself from the dashboard.

## Reports and exports

Reporting on the device shows the essentials. The dashboard has the full set: sales by period, item sales, category sales, employee sales and tips, taxes collected, payments by tender, refunds, discounts. Export any report to CSV. For automatic posting to QuickBooks, see [Clover QuickBooks integration](/clover/quickbooks-integration).

## Settings you should know exist

- **Receipts.** Setup, Receipts: logo, header and footer text, tip line, email and text options.
- **Tips.** Setup, Tips: presets, percentage or amount, on the customer screen or printed slip.
- **Taxes.** Setup, Taxes: rates and which items they apply to; tax-inclusive items.
- **Offline payments.** Setup, Payments, Offline Payments: enable and set a limit. Details on [Clover offline mode](/clover/offline-mode).
- **Order types.** Dine in, takeout, delivery, each with its own tax and printing behavior.
- **Scheduled restart.** Under device settings on newer firmware; a nightly restart keeps devices fast.

## What Clover cannot do from any of these apps

Change your processing rate, explain a fee, find a missing deposit, or cancel your account. Those are your processor's or dealer's. The split of who handles what is on the [Clover support page](/clover/support).
