## The one-device case

A very small counter without cash can run entirely on a Flex sitting in its dock, and take the Flex to events. A cafe that never leaves the counter can run entirely on a Mini. When in doubt between the two for a fixed counter, the Mini's larger screen and cash drawer support make it the safer single device.

## The two-device case

Restaurants with a dining room, retailers with curbside, and any business with a counter plus a road. Both devices sit on one account, one menu or inventory, one report. Dual pricing runs on both: the customer sees both totals on whichever screen is in front of them. The guest-side detail is on [dual pricing on Clover](/clover/dual-pricing).
