Not every business needs a point of sale. A chiropractor, a dry cleaner with a separate register, a tire shop that invoices from shop software, a church bookstore: they need a terminal that takes cards reliably and prints a receipt. Clover Compact is that terminal, on Clover's platform, without the POS fee. This page covers what it is and when to choose it over the Mini.

## Specs, September 2026

| Item | Clover Compact |
|---|---|
| Screen | 3.6-inch touchscreen |
| Printer | Built-in thermal receipt printer |
| Payments | EMV chip, swipe, NFC contactless (Apple Pay, Google Pay), PIN debit |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi and Ethernet; optional LTE |
| Build | Splash-resistant, end-to-end encryption |
| Software | Payments-only plan, $0 a month; dashboard reporting |
| Dual pricing | Supported; both totals on screen, price paid on receipt |
| Tips | On-screen tip prompt |
| Price | Dealer-sold; commonly placed at no charge for qualifying merchants |

## Who buys the Compact

- Professional and medical offices collecting copays and balances.
- Service businesses that quote and invoice from their own software and take the card at the counter.
- Retail with a legacy register that only needs a modern card terminal beside it.
- A second payment point in a store or restaurant that already has a Station.
- Anyone replacing a leased terminal from a previous processor. This is the most common case: a $40-a-month lease on a decade-old unit, swapped for a placed Compact.

## Dual pricing on a terminal

The Compact displays the cash total and card total, the customer chooses, the receipt shows the price paid and tender. Same compliance points as on any Clover: both prices visible before the choice, the card price charged as the price, no fee line. Signage at the counter completes it. Comparison to surcharging is on [dual pricing vs surcharging vs cash discount](/dual-pricing/dual-pricing-vs-surcharging-vs-cash-discount).

## Cost through a dealer

Placed at no charge for qualifying merchants, or purchased; never leased. No monthly software fee. Processing at $0 under dual pricing or on interchange-plus. A service business doing $15,000 a month in cards on a leased terminal at 2.9% is paying about $435 a month plus the lease; on a placed Compact with dual pricing it is paying nothing. The comparison method is on the [Clover pricing page](/clover/pricing).
