Station Solo is the same powerful station as the Duo with one screen instead of two. That saves $100 and a few inches of counter, and it costs two capabilities that matter more than the price. This page is about knowing which side of that trade you are on.

## Specs, September 2026

| Item | Station Solo |
|---|---|
| Screen | 14-inch IPS full HD touchscreen, staff-facing, with camera and barcode scanner |
| Customer screen | None |
| Printer | Built-in thermal receipt printer |
| Cash drawer | Included |
| Payments | Chip, swipe and PIN on the staff unit; customer tap requires a paired Flex or Mini |
| Connectivity | Ethernet, Wi-Fi, LTE |
| Apps | Full App Market; does not support semi-integrated third-party POS apps |
| List price | $1,799 outright; about $179 a month on a 36-month bundle |

## The two things it cannot do

**Customer tap.** Contactless payment on Clover happens on a customer-facing surface: the Duo's second screen, a Mini, or a Flex. The Solo's reader faces staff. A customer who wants to tap their phone at a Solo cannot, so nearly every Solo installation adds a Flex or Mini as the payment device. At that point the price gap to a Duo has closed.

**Semi-integrated apps.** Some third-party POS and vertical software connects to Clover in "semi-integrated" mode, using the Clover device as the payment terminal for their own software. The Duo supports that; the Solo does not. If your software vendor told you to get a Clover, ask which mode they use before choosing Solo.

## Where the Solo is right

- **Behind a bar.** The bartender rings, keeps tabs and closes out; guests tap on a Flex at the rail or sign a printed slip.
- **Host stand or server station.** Orders in, payments at the table on a Flex.
- **Back-office or admin station.** Reporting, inventory receiving, employee management, with payments elsewhere.
- **A second station** in a restaurant that already has a Duo at the counter.

## Where the Duo is right

Any counter where the customer faces the register: quick service, cafes, retail, any place with a tip prompt, and every counter running dual pricing, because the customer has to see both prices before choosing tender. The [dual pricing on Clover page](/clover/dual-pricing) explains why the customer-facing display matters for compliance. The full side-by-side is on [Station Duo vs Station Solo](/clover/station-duo-vs-solo).

## Cost through a dealer

Same as the Duo: placed at no charge for qualifying merchants or purchased outright, never leased; software at Clover's published plan prices; processing at $0 under dual pricing or on interchange-plus. Because the Solo usually needs a Flex or Mini beside it, quote the pair, not the station alone. Details on the [Clover pricing page](/clover/pricing).
